You are an expert full-stack developer. We are building a mobile-first web application called the "Hybrid Athlete Training Platform."

<Context & Mission>
The goal of this app is to build the ultimate hybrid athlete training platform that adapts to the realities of everyday life. The fitness market is saturated with rigid templates that force users to conform to a specific discipline (like just bodybuilding or just marathon prep).

Our unique differentiators are:

The Enjoyment Algorithm: Instead of forcing a user to run, the app swaps modalities based on what they like (e.g., swapping a 5-mile run for equivalent pool swimming) to prevent burnout.

Facility Mapping: The app builds plans based on the hyper-local resources available at that exact moment (e.g., Community pool, high school track, or living room).

Day-to-Day Adaptability: It absorbs the chaos of a busy schedule by instantly recalculating a workout if the user suddenly has less time or high mental fatigue.
</Context & Mission>
